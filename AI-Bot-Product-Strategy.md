## 7. Ad Strategy — Facebook & Instagram

### Campaign Structure

```
CAMPAIGN: AI Bot — Lead Gen
      │
      ├── AD SET 1: Cold — Turf Business Owners (Phoenix, LV, Dallas, SD, Austin)
      │     Targeting: Business owners | Home services | Landscaping industry
      │     Age: 28–55
      │     Budget: $5/day
      │
      └── AD SET 2: Retargeting — Video viewers 50%+
            Budget: $2/day
```

### The 3 Ad Angles That Work in This Space

**Angle 1: The Speed Demo (Highest performer)**
Show the bot responding to a lead in real time. Screen recording. No pitch. Just show it working.

Hook: *"I was on a job at 6pm when this lead came in..."*
Video: Screen record of a customer texting → bot responding in 15 seconds → quote delivered → customer says "let's book it"
CTA: "Want this in your business? Comment 'BOT' or tap below."

**Angle 2: The Pain Point**
Call out the exact moment they lose money.

Hook: *"You're losing $1,000/week to leads you never follow up on. Here's proof."*
Content: Walk through the math. A missed lead at 9pm = $250 gone. 4 per week = $1,000. Bot costs $300/month. ROI is obvious.
CTA: "Text us to see how it works."

**Angle 3: The Founder Story**
Most credible angle. Authenticity wins on Meta and TikTok.

Hook: *"I built an AI bot for my own turf cleaning company. Then other owners started asking me how to get one."*
Content: Brief story of building it for SCT, results it generated, now making it available.
CTA: "DM me or tap below to learn more."

## 8. Ad Strategy — TikTok

TikTok is ideal for this because:
- Business owners scroll TikTok
- "AI for my business" content performs extremely well organically
- Low competition in the turf/home service niche

### Content Formats

**Video 1: The Live Demo**
Film your phone. Open a fresh conversation. Show the bot receiving a message, generating a quote, and sending it — in real time. Text overlay: "This AI bot closes jobs while I sleep." No editing needed.

**Video 2: The Results Screen**
Screenshot or screen record of your GHL conversation list showing multiple booked clients. Voiceover: "Every one of these bookings happened automatically. The AI handled it."

**Video 3: The Myth Buster**
"5 things I thought I needed to grow my service business (that I was wrong about)" — #5 is "a full-time receptionist" → replaced by AI.

**Video 4: Day in the Life**
Morning routine. You're on a job. Phone buzzes — new booking. "I didn't do anything. The bot did." Raw, real, relatable.

### TikTok Organic Strategy (Free)
Before running paid TikTok ads, post 2–3 organic videos first. If one gets traction, put $50–100 behind it as a Spark Ad (promoting the organic post). This is the most cost-efficient TikTok strategy at your budget.

## 9. The Click-to-Text Funnel (Same Principle, New Audience)

Your ad CTA drives them to text YOU about the product.

**Your click-to-text link:**
```
sms:+1YOURSALESNUMBER?body=Hi, I want to learn more about the AI sales bot for my business
```

When they text, you (or a simple qualifying bot) respond:
- "Hey! What type of service business do you run?"
- Get basic info: location, monthly leads, current follow-up process
- Schedule a 15-min demo call, OR send a Loom video demo

This mirrors exactly what you're selling — and shows them the product in action the moment they reach out.

## 10. The Sales Demo

The most powerful close is showing them the bot working.

**Demo script:**
1. "Text this number right now like you're a customer looking for a quote."
2. Let them experience it themselves.
3. Ask: "How long does it currently take you to respond to a new lead?"
4. "This just did it in 15 seconds. What would it mean for your business if every lead got that?"

No slide deck needed. The product sells itself when you let them feel it.

## 11. Launch Sequence (30 Days)

**Week 1 — Foundation**
- Finalize brand name + domain
- Create a simple one-page website or landing page (headline, demo video, pricing, CTA)
- Set up your own sales SMS number in GHL (separate from SCT)
- Create click-to-text link for your own product

**Week 2 — Content**
- Film 3 TikTok videos (demo, results, founder story)
- Create 2 Meta ad creatives (speed demo + pain point angle)
- Post TikTok organically — watch which gets traction

**Week 3 — Launch Ads**
- Launch Meta campaign ($7/day)
- If a TikTok video is getting views, boost it as Spark Ad ($3/day)
- All clicks → your click-to-text number
- You handle demos personally at first (don't automate sales yet)

**Week 4 — First Clients**
- Close your first 3–5 paying clients
- Onboard them: connect their GHL, customize bot training, test flow
- Get testimonials/results screenshots immediately
- Feed those into next round of ads
