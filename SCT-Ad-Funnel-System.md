## Funnel Overview

```
AD (Meta or TikTok)
      ↓
CLICK-TO-TEXT CTA → Opens SMS thread on phone
      ↓
GHL WEBHOOK → AI Sales Bot takes over
      ↓
BOT QUALIFIES → Gets address + sqft → Quotes price
      ↓
BOOKS or ESCALATES TO DAVE
      ↓
24-HR NUDGE (if no response)
```

No website needed. No form fills. No manual follow-up. The ad drops them directly into your AI bot.

## Step 1 — Set Up the Click-to-Text Link

This is the single most important setup task. Your AI bot is already live — you just need ads to feed it.

**Create your click-to-text link:**
```
sms:+1YOURNUMBER?body=Hi, I saw your ad and want a quote for my turf!
```

Replace `+1YOURNUMBER` with your GHL phone number (the one that triggers the webhook). When someone taps your ad's CTA button, their phone opens a pre-filled text message ready to send. The moment they hit send, your AI bot responds.

**On Meta:** Set your ad's CTA to "Send Message" → "SMS" and paste the link.
**On TikTok:** Use the website URL field with your click-to-text link, or "Message" CTA if available in your ad type.

**Test it yourself first.** Click the link on your phone, make sure the AI responds, and walk through the full quote flow before spending a dollar.

## Step 3 — Meta Ad Setup (Facebook + Instagram)

### Campaign Structure

```
CAMPAIGN: SCT — Lead Gen (Conversion Objective)
  │
  ├── AD SET 1: Phoenix Homeowners — Cold
  │     Targeting: 10–25 mile radius around Phoenix
  │     Age: 30–65 | Homeowners only
  │     Interests: Home improvement, landscaping, outdoor living
  │     Budget: $5/day
  │
  └── AD SET 2: Video Viewers — Retargeting
        Audience: People who watched 50%+ of your TikTok/Reel videos
        Budget: $2/day
```

### Ad Creative — 3 Formats to Rotate

**Format 1: Before/After Split (Static Image)**
- Left: Dirty, matted, discolored turf
- Right: Same turf looking fresh and clean
- Headline: "Your turf is dirtier than you think."
- Subtext: "Phoenix's #1 artificial turf cleaning service. Get a quote in 60 seconds."
- CTA Button: "Send Message" → click-to-text

**Format 2: Short Reel (15–30 seconds)**
Hook (first 3 seconds — text overlay + voiceover):
> "This Phoenix homeowner thought their turf was fine… until we showed them this."

Content: Time-lapse or side-by-side cleaning footage. Show the grime, show the result.

Closing text overlay:
> "Tap below to get your price — takes 60 seconds"

CTA: click-to-text

**Format 3: Testimonial / Social Proof**
- Screenshot or quote from a happy customer (with permission)
- Overlay text: "138 Phoenix homeowners cleaned this year"
- CTA: "What does it cost for your yard? Text us now."

### Copy Formula (works for all formats)
1. **Hook** — Call out a problem or fear ("Your kids are playing on bacteria-loaded turf")
2. **Credibility** — Brief proof ("We've cleaned 100+ yards in Phoenix")
3. **Offer** — Make it easy ("Get a price in 60 seconds, no sales call")
4. **CTA** — One action ("Tap 'Send Message' and text us your address")

## Step 4 — TikTok Ad Setup

### Campaign Type
**Reach or Video Views objective** — not conversions yet. TikTok's pixel needs data before conversion campaigns work efficiently at $3/day.

### Targeting
- Location: Phoenix metro, 25-mile radius
- Age: 25–55
- Interests: Home & Garden, DIY, Outdoor Living
- No lookalikes yet — broad is fine at this budget

### Content That Works on TikTok

TikTok rewards authenticity over polish. Your before/after content is perfect here.

**Video ideas (film these with your phone):**

1. **The Reveal** — Camera slowly pans across dirty turf, then cut to clean. Text overlay: "We clean artificial turf in Phoenix and the results are insane." No script needed.

2. **The Process** — 30-second timelapse of a job from start to finish. Add trending audio. Text: "This is what we do to your turf."

3. **The Myth Buster** — "Most people think artificial turf doesn't need cleaning. Here's why that's wrong." Walk through 3 quick reasons (bacteria, pet waste, allergens).

4. **The Price Reveal** — "How much does it cost to clean artificial turf in Phoenix?" → Brief explanation → "Text us your sqft and we'll tell you in 60 seconds."

**TikTok CTA:** Add your click-to-text URL as the website link on your profile and in your video captions. TikTok ads can link directly to it.

## Step 5 — Retargeting Layer (Week 3–4 Onward)

Once you have 500+ video views (usually by week 2–3), build a retargeting audience:

**Meta:**
- Custom Audience → Video → "People who watched 50% of your videos"
- Run a separate $2/day ad set to this group with a stronger offer:
  > "Still thinking about it? Book this week and lock in our 15% first-time special."
- CTA: click-to-text

**TikTok:**
- Custom Audience → "Video viewers 75%+"
- Same concept — warmer message since they've seen you before

This retargeting layer is often where the cheapest leads come from because the audience already knows you.

## Step 6 — Tracking & Optimization

### What to Track Weekly

| Metric | Where to Find It | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Cost per lead (CPL) | Meta Ads Manager | Under $35 |
| Lead-to-quote rate | GHL conversations | 80%+ |
| Quote-to-book rate | GHL / manual count | 40–70% |
| Cost per acquisition | CPL ÷ close rate | Under $75 |
| Nudge conversion rate | GHL nudge logs | Track weekly |

### 30-Day Optimization Cycle

**Week 1:** Launch. Don't touch anything. Let Meta's algorithm learn.

**Week 2:** Check CPM and CTR only. If CTR is below 0.8%, swap the creative hook. Everything else — leave it.

**Week 3:** Check cost per lead. If above $50, tighten the audience (remove broad interests, keep homeowner targeting). Also check if the AI bot is converting leads — if people are texting but not booking, the problem is the bot, not the ads.

**Week 4:** Cut the underperforming ad set. Reallocate budget to the winner. Add retargeting campaign.

**Month 2:** Once you have consistent lead flow, test a lookalike audience built from your booked-customer list (upload phone numbers/emails to Meta as a Custom Audience, then create a 1% lookalike).

## Step 7 — The Offer

At $10/day you can't out-spend competitors. You win on the offer and the speed of follow-up.

**Recommended ad offer:**
> "15% off your first cleaning — get your price in 60 seconds, no phone call needed."

This does three things:
1. Removes friction (no phone call, just text)
2. Creates urgency (first-time special)
3. Qualifies intent (if they text, they're a warm lead)

Your AI bot already uses the "15% special" language — this keeps the messaging consistent from ad to close.

## Notes

- **Your AI bot is the unfair advantage.** Competitors run the same ads but take hours to follow up. You respond in seconds, 24/7. That alone improves close rate significantly.
- **Don't scale budget until CPL is proven.** Get to a repeatable $25–35 CPL first, then scale.
- **Content is the limiting factor at this budget.** Film before/after content every single job. That's your creative fuel.
- **The 24-hour nudge handles the fence-sitters.** Leads that don't book immediately get one automated follow-up. Don't add more — one nudge, then move on.
